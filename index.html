Welcome to Kigali

The cleanest city in Africa — full of culture, food, and unforgettable experiences.

Why Visit Kigali?

Kigali is the capital city of Rwanda. It is known for being very clean, safe, and beautiful. The city sits on many hills and has a lot to offer — from history and art to amazing food and nightlife.

Top Reasons to Visit:

  • One of the cleanest cities in Africa
  • Amazing local and international food
  • Rich art and culture scene
  • Gateway to gorilla trekking in Volcanoes National Park
  • Modern infrastructure and friendly people
  • Powerful history and memorials
