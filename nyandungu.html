Nyandungu Eco Park

Kigali's peaceful urban wetland and nature escape

About Nyandungu Eco Park

Nyandungu Urban Wetland Eco Park is a beautiful green space located in the eastern part of Kigali. It was opened in 2021 and quickly became one of the most loved places in the city.

The park covers about 120 hectares and was built to protect the natural wetland that existed there. It is home to hundreds of plant species, birds, and small animals. Walking through Nyandungu feels like stepping out of the busy city into a calm, green world.

The park has well-maintained walking paths, wooden bridges over the wetland, picnic areas, and lookout points. It is the perfect place to relax, exercise, or simply enjoy nature without leaving Kigali.

Top Things To Do at Nyandungu

  1. Walk the Nature Trails — Follow the well-marked paths through the wetland and forest areas. The main trail takes about 1–2 hours.
  2. Birdwatching — Over 100 bird species have been spotted here. Bring binoculars for the best experience.
  3. Cross the Wooden Bridges — Several beautiful wooden bridges cross over the wetland — great for photos.
  4. Have a Picnic — Use the designated picnic areas surrounded by trees and fresh air.
  5. Photography — The park is stunning — from close-up flowers to wide wetland views.
  6. Exercise — Many Kigali residents come here for morning jogs and yoga sessions.

Wildlife You Might See

  • Grey Crowned Crane — Rwanda's national bird
  • African Jacana — walks on floating lily pads
  • Various frog and toad species
  • Dozens of butterfly species
  • Rare insects and beetles
  • Over 200 plant and flower species
  • 🌿\ Papyrus reeds and water lilies
