About Fazenda Sengha

Fazenda Sengha is a popular outdoor adventure park on Mount Kigali, offering a mix of nature, fun, and relaxation just a short drive from the city center. Visitors can enjoy hors e riding, zip-lining, quad biking, archery, hiking trails, and scenic views over Kigali. It’s a great destination for families, friends, and adventure lovers

What To Do at Fazenda Sengha

  1. Horse Riding — Explore trails on horseback with beautiful views
  2. Zip-lining — Adventure through rugged outdoor paths
  3. Quad Biking — Adventure through rugged outdoor paths
  4. Archery — Try target shooting in a fun outdoor setting
  5. Nature Walks & Picnics — Relax, hike, and enjoy Kigali’s scenic hills
  6. Restaurant — Relax, eat, and enjoy views over Kigali
