Fazenda Sengha

A working farm, eco-lodge and retreat just outside Kigali

About Fazenda Sengha

Fazenda Sengha is a unique agri-tourism destination located a short drive from Kigali city centre. "Fazenda" is a Portuguese word meaning farm, and this place truly lives up to that name — it is a working farm that welcomes visitors to experience rural Rwandan life.

The property features beautiful rolling hills, coffee and tea plantations, vegetable gardens, livestock, and comfortable accommodation. It is a perfect escape from the busy city — while still being very close to Kigali.

Fazenda Sengha is great for families, couples, school trips, and anyone who wants to understand where their food comes from and how traditional Rwandan farming works.

What To Do at Fazenda Sengha

  1. Farm Tour — Walk through the coffee, tea, and vegetable plantations with a guide who explains how everything is grown.
  2. Coffee Experience — Learn the full journey of Rwandan coffee from picking the cherry to drinking a cup.
  3. Cooking Class — Learn to cook traditional Rwandan meals using fresh ingredients picked from the farm.
  4. Animal Feeding — Meet and feed the farm animals — great for children and families.
  5. Hiking — Explore the surrounding hills with breathtaking views of the Kigali landscape.
  6. Stay Overnight — Comfortable eco-lodge rooms let you wake up in the peaceful countryside.
